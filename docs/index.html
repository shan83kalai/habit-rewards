Habit Rewards Family

Daily habits, turned into pocket money

An iPhone app for parents.

  • Tick each child's habits every day: done or missed.
  • Each day is scored automatically, and the month adds up to their pocket money.
  • Give each child their own habits, and add one-off extra tasks, like washing the car, with their own reward.
  • See the whole month as a grid, mark payments as paid, and export to a spreadsheet or PDF.
  • Share with the other parent through iCloud, so both phones stay in step.
  • No accounts, no ads, no tracking.
